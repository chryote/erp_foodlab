## Feature: HR / EmployeeAttendance
- **Table:** `employee_attendances`
  - `id` (`bigIncrements`)
  - `company_id` (`foreignId` -> `companies.id`, `restrictOnDelete()`)
  - `employee_id` (`foreignId` -> `employees.id`, `restrictOnDelete()`)
  - `employee_shift_id` (`foreignId` -> `employee_shifts.id`, nullable, `restrictOnDelete()`)
  - `attendance_date` (`date`)
  - `clock_in_at` (`timestamp`, nullable)
  - `clock_out_at` (`timestamp`, nullable)
  - `late_minutes` (`unsignedSmallInteger`, default `0`)
  - `overtime_minutes` (`unsignedSmallInteger`, default `0`)
  - `status` (`string(30)`, default `'present'`)
  - `notes` (`string(500)`, nullable)
  - `timestamps()`, `softDeletes()`
- **Schema & DB Guardrails:**
  - **Money / Totals:** N/A (if overtime pay is stored later, use `decimal(18, 2)`)
  - **Tenant & FKs:** Scoped to `company_id`; explicit FKs on `company_id`, `employee_id`, and `employee_shift_id`, all with `restrictOnDelete()`
  - **Soft-Delete Uniqueness:** Generated `active_flag` (`unsignedTinyInteger()->nullable()->storedAs('IF(deleted_at IS NULL, 1, NULL)')`, add `'active_flag'` to Model `$hidden`); `unique(['company_id', 'employee_id', 'attendance_date', 'active_flag'])`
  - **Indexing:** Composite `(company_id, deleted_at)`, `(company_id, attendance_date)`, and `(employee_id, attendance_date)`; no standalone indexes on `created_at`, `updated_at`, `deleted_at`, or `status`
- **Enum (`app/Enums/`):** `AttendanceStatusEnum: present | late | absent | leave | sick | holiday`
- **Relations:** `belongsTo(Company::class)`, `belongsTo(Employee::class)`, `belongsTo(EmployeeShift::class)`
- **Endpoints (`/api/v1/modules/hr/employee-attendances`):** `index`, `store`, `show`, `update`, `destroy`
- **QueryBuilder (`Service::list`):** filters=[`company_id`, `employee_id`, `employee_shift_id`, `attendance_date`, `status`], sorts=[`attendance_date`, `clock_in_at`, `late_minutes`, `overtime_minutes`], includes=[`company`, `employee`, `employeeShift`]
- **Safety / Business Rules:**
  - Validate `employee_id` and `employee_shift_id` belong to the same `company_id`
  - Validate `clock_out_at >= clock_in_at` when both are provided
  - `RecordAttendanceAction` inside `DB::transaction()` to compute `late_minutes` (respecting shift `grace_period_minutes`) and `overtime_minutes`
- **Seeder (`database/seeders/DatabaseSeeder.php`):** idempotent sample attendance rows for seeded employees (`updateOrCreate` / `withTrashed()` on `(company_id, employee_id, attendance_date)` inside `DB::transaction()`, end with `Cache::flush()`); assert `employee_attendances` populated in `SystemIntegrityTest`
- **Pest Test (`tests/Feature/EmployeeAttendanceTest.php`):** CRUD, filters, 422 validation (cross-company employee/shift mismatch, invalid clock-out time), soft delete, active-duplicate `(employee_id, attendance_date)` rejection + re-create after soft delete
